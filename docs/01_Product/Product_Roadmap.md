# Product Roadmap

## Purpose

Dokumen ini menjelaskan rencana pengembangan Temenin Ajaa berdasarkan prioritas bisnis dan kesiapan teknologi.

---

# Product Vision

Roadmap berfokus pada membangun platform companion premium yang stabil sebelum melakukan ekspansi fitur.

---

# Version 1.0 — Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

Target:
Meluncurkan aplikasi pertama yang siap digunakan oleh Customer, Partner, dan Admin.

### Customer

- Authentication
- Profile
- Ride
- Hangout
- Freedom Request
- Multi Service Booking
- Itinerary
- Add-on
- Checkout
- Payment
- Booking History
- Rating & Review
- Notification

### Partner

- Registration
- Verification
- Availability
- Booking Management
- Earnings
- Rating
- Profile

### Admin

- Dashboard
- Booking Management
- Partner Management
- Customer Management
- Pricing Management
- Review Moderation

---

# Version 1.1

Target:
Meningkatkan retensi pengguna.

### Features

- Favorite Partner
- Promo
- Voucher
- Referral
- Push Notification Improvement
- Analytics Dashboard
- Cancellation Improvement

# Version 2.0

Target:

- Dynamic Pricing
- Subscription

# Version 3.0

Target:
Ekspansi nasional.

- Partner Level
- API Integration

---

# Prioritization Framework

Priority menggunakan metode:

- P0 — Critical
- P1 — High
- P2 — Medium
- P3 — Low

---

# Success Criteria

- MVP stabil
- Crash Rate < 1%
- Booking Success > 95%
- Customer Rating > 4.8
